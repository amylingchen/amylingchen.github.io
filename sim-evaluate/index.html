pi0.5 closed-loop evaluation replays

The “Deployment scene” group is the same step-10000 model evaluated in 10 new backgrounds: 8–11 standing people, 2–4 tables and 1–3 UR5e arms per scene, with a video for every episode. The other groups have a result for every episode but a video only for every 5th. Success means the policy released the peg by itself and the peg sits at the bottom of the hole. Videos show, left to right, the D455, the wrist camera and the left L515, played at 5 fps, about 2× real time. Peak force is the highest force within the episode; the real robot stops at 25 N. These are sim results: the sim-trained models have seen this kind of image, the real-only model has not.
